Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

SocialPilot OS is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no server operated by the developer, and the developer does not receive your data.

What the app receives from TikTok

Only after you sign in on TikTok's page and give permission:

  • Account identifier, display name, username and profile information (user.info.basic, user.info.profile)
  • Follower, like and video counts (user.info.stats)
  • Your public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts (video.list)
  • Permission to send videos you approve to your TikTok inbox (video.upload)
  • Access tokens that keep the connection working

How it is used

To show your channel statistics, compute growth, create reports and alerts, and send your approved videos to TikTok. It is not used for advertising, profiling other people, or training AI models, and it is not sold.

Where it is stored

In a folder on your computer. Tokens are kept in a separate file that only your user account can read. They are not included in logs or reports.

Sharing

The app sends data to TikTok in order to work. If you optionally add a YouTube API key, trend searches are sent to Google. No TikTok data is shared with anyone else.

Keeping and deleting

Per-video statistics are kept for 40 days and daily channel totals for up to 400 days; weekly reports for about half a year. Removing an account in the app deletes its tokens and asks TikTok to revoke them. Scheduled posts, the activity log and statistics stay on your computer until you delete the app's data folder. You can also revoke access from your TikTok account settings.

Contact

For questions, open an issue in this site's GitHub repository. Do not include personal information in an issue. Changes to this policy are posted on this page.
